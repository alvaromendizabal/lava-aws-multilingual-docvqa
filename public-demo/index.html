01 / Collection2 documents
What is indexed?

Native text only. Physical page numbers include covers and unavailable pages.

How this works
  1. Normalize Unicode and tokenize words plus character bigrams/trigrams.
  2. Rank every physical page with BM25.
  3. Rank passages inside the evidence window and copy one verbatim.
  4. Validate schema, supplied citations and exact source text.

Coverage is lexical overlap, not a calibrated probability. Copying a sentence does not prove it answers the question.
