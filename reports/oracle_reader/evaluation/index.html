Coverage and interpretation

The frozen set has 15 Japanese questions and one Vietnamese question. Language is confounded with document identity. The Vietnamese result is a single example, not a language-level estimate.

These are frozen-model descriptive evaluations. The existence of nested fold manifests does not mean nested training, tuning, or cross-validation has been performed. Retrieval is held at oracle evidence; overall oracle diagnostics contain a fixed grounding contribution.
